    🧭 How MileMates works

    1. Route: the road route comes from OSRM on OpenStreetMap data. Drive times get a 20% buffer for Indian traffic, ghats and towns.
    2. Timing: choose “Leave at” to fix your start time, or “Reach by” to fix your arrival time, and MileMates works out the other end. Each day can be changed separately with ✏️, and you get a warning if you'd arrive after sunset.
    3. Days: the drive is split so no day goes over your “max driving per day”. Night halts are snapped to real towns near each split point. Spare days become explore days at your destination.
    4. Weather: uses the live forecast for trips in the next 16 days. Further out, it shows what the weather was on the same dates last year.
    5. Stays: hotels, homestays, hostels and camps listed on OpenStreetMap near each night halt. Booking buttons open Booking.com, MakeMyTrip, Airbnb or Google Maps with your town, dates and guests filled in.
    6. Costs: fuel = distance ÷ mileage × fuel price × vehicles. Rooms are for 2 people each, tolls are a rough ₹/km estimate, and every rate can be edited.
    7. Privacy: there are no accounts and no servers of our own. Your trips stay in your browser.
